# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We aim for a friendly, respectful and harassment-free community for everyone,
regardless of experience level, identity or background.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contribute to a positive environment:

- Being respectful and constructive in feedback
- Showing empathy and assuming good faith
- Accepting criticism and differing opinions

Unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, intimidation or personal attacks
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments
- Publishing others' private information without permission

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported to the maintainers privately
(see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) for the contact address). All reports are
reviewed and handled confidentially.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies to the project repositories, issue tracker, pull
requests and related community spaces.
